# Changelog

This fork adapts Odysseus into a student-focused study assistant and academic productivity workspace.

## Main Goals

- Design the UI for student study and academic assistance.
- Manage and organize schedules, tasks, documents, and projects.
- Track project progress, deadlines, and related academic work.
- Organize school email in one place.
- Automatically create calendar events and to-do items from school emails.
